# Statement of purpose — shared draft

Write the research narrative once here, then copy into each school's `statements/` folder and tailor faculty, labs, and length.

## Target length

Check each portal. Common ranges: 1–3 pages; some schools hard-cap at 2 pages single-spaced.

## Outline

1. **Opening claim** — the research problem you want to spend a PhD on
2. **Preparation** — 2–3 projects with technical depth (what you built/proved, what failed, what you learned)
3. **Agenda** — 2–3 concrete questions for the next 5 years
4. **Fit** — 2–4 faculty and why their recent papers match your agenda (school-specific)
5. **Close** — career aim and why this department

Do not reuse the personal-history / personal statement here. Those cover path, access, and identity; this file covers research.
