# Personal / history statement — shared draft

Many programs require a **second** essay that is not the SOP (personal history, diversity, or life experience). Copy the relevant prompt from each portal into that school's `statements/` folder.

## Shared outline

1. Formative experiences that shaped your path into research
2. Obstacles, access, or context (optional, if relevant and asked)
3. How that history helps you succeed in graduate study and contribute to the community
4. Keep research agenda details in the SOP

## Tips

- Follow word/page caps exactly
- Some portals want a header (document type, name, program, ID)
- Do not duplicate the SOP research narrative here
